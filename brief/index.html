Briefs

Concise analysis of international affairs, legal issues and policy questions.

Briefs are designed to communicate an issue, its context, relevant evidence and possible implications in a clear and accessible format.

Unlike longer research projects, these pieces focus on developing the ability to identify important issues and communicate analysis concisely.
